How to use this: chapters with more M + D year marks are a sensible place to begin revision. Each mark is a printed topic/year reference, not necessarily one unique MCQ; a year may be listed under multiple topics. Labels marked MAT are excluded from these M + D totals. This is a study guide based on the supplied source, not a guarantee of future questions.

How to read the counts: the PDF lists year references (such as M: 23-24 or D: 23-24), not a complete numeric count of individual MCQs. The number shown here is the count of printed label + year references. A topic with no printed years is marked Unverified, not zero. These totals are not guaranteed to represent unique questions.
